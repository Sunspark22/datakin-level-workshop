DATAKIN · ROM EDITION

Bring your own cartridge

Supply your own original Datakin: Server Delver ROM (v0.79 or v0.78) to open its stages. The game is available from CircuitFoxes on itch.io.

  1. Put Datakin-Server-Delver.gba in the editor's rom folder.
  2. Use the original ROM, not a previously edited export. README.md lists the supported SHA-256.
  3. Check ROM and open workshop
